Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

muraemon ("the Developer") provides this Privacy Policy to explain how user information is handled in the mobile application "Custom Bingo" ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

The App may collect the following information through third-party services:

  • Advertising identifiers (Advertising ID / IDFA): Collected by Google AdMob for the purpose of serving ads.
  • Purchase information: Collected by RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. This includes purchase history and subscription status. Payment details such as credit card numbers are processed by Google Play / the App Store and are never shared with the Developer or RevenueCat.

Bingo lists and cards you create in the App are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

2. How We Use Information

  • To serve advertisements and measure their effectiveness
  • To manage and restore in-app subscription purchases
  • To improve the quality of the App

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (subscription management): RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Except for the services listed above, the Developer does not share your personal information with any third party.

4. Data Storage and Deletion

  • Data created in the App is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.
  • Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled from your Google Play or App Store account settings.
  • Advertising identifiers can be reset or restricted from your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised in response to legal changes or new features of the App. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
